Build stronger portfolios with UBS Core ETFs

Strong portfolios should start with strong foundations. We believe that our UBS Core ETFs will give you the building blocks you need to strengthen the core of your portfolio.

UBS Core ETFs have the potential to provide you with a resilient and cost-efficient investment. Whether you are seeking long-term growth, stability or a combination of both these Core ETFs may deliver you high-quality exposure, broad diversification, and the trusted expertise you expect at low cost.

When investing internationally and holding assets in currencies other than your own, portfolios are exposed to currency risks. Exchange rate fluctuations can reduce or increase returns; that’s why currency hedging has become an essential tool for mitigating this risk.
