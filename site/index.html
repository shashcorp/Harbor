Methodology

Performance

Daily closes from Hyperliquid candleSnapshot for every constituent, weighted by the fund's target weights and rebalanced daily. Windows are measured to 2 November 2026 and are gross of fees. A window is only published when at least 90% of NAV has a price at its start date — that is why equity funds, whose HIP-3 markets listed between March and September 2026, show n/a for YTD or 1Y.

Rating

HLX model rating on a 1–5 scale, built from the simulated Sharpe ratio (risk-free 3%), maximum drawdown and annualised volatility over the fund's full simulated history. It is a within-range relative score, not an absolute quality judgement, and no external agency rates these strategies.

Liquidity grade

A: ≥90% of NAV in markets trading over $1m/day. B: ≥70%. C: below that — these need limit orders, smaller clips and patience. Thin positions are marked in each portfolio table.

Simulated strategies. HLX funds are not registered collective investment schemes and are not offered to the public. Performance is a backtest of the stated target weights on historical Hyperliquid market data — not the return of any actual portfolio. n/a marks a window that starts before a material constituent was listed. Model ratings are computed by HLX and are not Morningstar, Lipper or S&P ratings.
